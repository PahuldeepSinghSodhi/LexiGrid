    EASY TO START. FUN TO MASTER.

    A few simple rules.

    1. Connect neighbouring letters.

      Hold and drag sideways, vertically, or diagonally. You can also tap tiles and press Submit or Enter.

    2. Change your mind.

      Tap a selected tile to remove it and all later letters. Hold and retrace to remove later letters while keeping the tile you returned to. Undo or Backspace removes the last letter; Clear or Escape removes everything.

    3. Build a word with 3 or more letters.

      Use each tile once within a word. Release your drag to submit it. A word only scores once per round. Rejected words clear automatically so you can start again.

    4. Longer words. Bigger scores.

      3 / 4 / 5 / 6 / 7+ letters earn 100 / 400 / 800 / 1,200 / 2,000 base points.

    5. Keep your momentum.

      Time since your last accepted word: under 5s gets 1.5×; 5 to under 10s gets 1.25×; 10s or more gets base points. Each pair of distinct accepted words under 5 seconds apart also adds 2 seconds. Invalid or repeated submissions break the pair, and the bonus cannot revive an expired round.
